About

Hi, I'm Cynthia

I'm currently a senior in high school, attending Sentinel Secondary School. I have always loved food, cooking, and baking, and over time I became more curious about the nutritional implications of what I was cooking and eating. That sparked my interest in the field of food and nutrition and the start of this blog. I hope that through this project, I can expand my scope of knowledge on this topic and potentially pursue post-secondary education studying nutrition.

Why this blog?

NutriNotes is my capstone project. Every recipe comes with a nutrition breakdown and a “nutrition spotlight” that explains one idea about what the food does in your body.

I hope that with this blog, in addition to expanding my own scope of knowledge on nutrition, I can inspire others to take initiative in their nutrition, learn more about what is in food, and grow interest in the field of food and nutrition.

A quick note

Nutrition values are estimates for learning purposes, not medical advice.
